ZED — INSTALL ON YOUR IPHONE (no Mac, no Xcode, free)
=====================================================

Zed installs from Safari like an app: own Home Screen icon, full screen,
works offline, keeps your playlists, and shows on the lock screen.
It just needs to be put on the web once so your iPhone can reach it.

STEP 1 — Put it online (one time, ~5 minutes, on your PC)
  1. Unzip this folder.
  2. Go to github.com and make a free account (skip if you have one).
  3. Click "+" (top right) > "New repository". Name it: zed
     Set it to Public. Click "Create repository".
  4. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in ALL the files from the
     unzipped folder (index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest,
     jszip.min.js and the 3 .png icons). Click "Commit changes".
  5. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)",
     click Save. Wait about 1 minute.
  6. Your app address will be:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/zed/

STEP 2 — Install on your iPhone
  1. Open that address in SAFARI (must be Safari).
  2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow) > "Add to Home Screen" > Add.
  3. Open Zed from the Home Screen icon from now on — not from Safari.
     (The Home Screen app keeps its own separate library.)

USING IT
  - "+ Add songs" opens the Files app — pick MP3s from iCloud Drive /
    On My iPhone. Zed copies them in, so they stay even if you move
    the originals.
  - Volume: use the iPhone's side buttons (iOS doesn't let web apps
    set volume, so the slider is hidden there).
  - Lock screen / Control Center show the song with play, pause,
    skip and scrub.
  - "Export .zip" opens the share sheet — choose "Save to Files".

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Songs are stored on your phone only. Deleting the Zed icon deletes
    its library.
  - Lots of music uses storage; check Settings > General > iPhone Storage.
  - Updating: re-upload a changed file to GitHub, and change
    'zed-v1' to 'zed-v2' in sw.js so phones pick up the new version.
